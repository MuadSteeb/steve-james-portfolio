Hi! I'm Steve. Thanks for visiting my site.

I am a San Francisco-based creative production leader with 20+ years of experience spanning live events, theater, video production, broadcast media, digital content, interactive installations, and emerging technologies.

In 2022 I founded an art and tech collective called Supermagenta. We're working on some short films and video games, but it's all very hush hush.

Most recently I have been leading content production for events at GitHub, among other things.

Hey, check this out...

In 2024 we filmed a little documentary drama about producing GitHub Universe. I'm featured in it quite a bit.

For the past eight years, I have been leading content production for events at GitHub.

This includes the annual flagship GitHub Universe event. My oversight includes space planning and production technology design and implementation for all stages, livestreams, video production spaces, and other onsite production. I also play executive producer or producer role in all production happening onsite, including marquee executive keynotes, breakout sessions, livestreams, podcasts, sizzle reels, etc. I also play producer for much of the design and motion graphic treatment that happens on screens throughout the venue (or venues), but sometimes I think the actual designers are just humoring me.

I also lead production on larger internal company events, including Company All-Hands, Revenue Kick Off (RKO) events, and exclusive Senior Leadership Summits.

And I produce and direct video and motion graphic packages outside of live event world. Whatever needs doing. I guess I stay pretty busy.

Some recent work...

A few recent projects across the gamut of production disciplines

Live Keynote

Universe Day 2 Keynote - 2025

Executive Producer, Production Lead

I led design of the theater space from an empty shell of a building into a full keynote experience, including tech specifications and implementation, live demo tech, and livestreaming. Also played content producer for the session itself. This theater was designed to split into multiple breakout stages after the opening keynote.

Virtual Keynote

GitHub Galaxy Virtual Keynote - 2023

Executive Producer, Director

Galaxy was a series of virtual events aimed primarily at an enterprise audience. This keynote was filmed with a small crew in GitHub's Seattle office on a tight budget and production schedule. We got scrappy with the motion graphics and used slide animations exported as video files with transparent backgrounds.

Customer Story

AMD x GitHub Customer Story - 2025

Producer, Director

This customer story was filmed over the course of two days at AMD's main office in Toronto. It was a visually and acoustically challenging mainly-cubicles office so we filmed the majority of this across one atrium common space and one conference room. Cool people and a great Toronto-based crew.

Product Launch

GitHub Copilot App Launch - 2026

Producer, Director

This came in very hot and was planned with only a few days notice and filmed in under three hours with a small, nimble crew in GitHub's San Francisco office. Product UI was rigged very quickly in a combination of the actual product and After Effects while the piece itself was being edited.

AI-assisted video

RKO Keynote Opening Animation - 2025

Producer, Director

A pre-Keynote hype video made primarily with AI prompt engineering. The product UI was rigged with After Effects. All other visuals were rendered with a node-based AI video workflow running local models. AI video generation has come a long way and is a pretty amazing tool for certain use cases.

Event Recap

GitHub Universe Event Recap Sizzle - 2026

Executive Producer

A post-event recap sizzle intended as part content key message reiteration, part fomo inducer, part promo for the same event next year. Filmed entirely during the two-day event and delivered the next day by agency partners. The more production executing concurrently, the more broad the management pyramid.

Podcast

TQ Ventures Podcast - 2026

Executive Producer, Director

A short social media clip of a much longer podcast-format show. This was the pilot episode, filmed as a one-off in a rental location. We wanted to keep it light and conversational, as opposed to getting into full podcast-studio SM7B territory.

Startup Launch

Tuva AI company launch - 2026

Executive Producer, Director

Tech startup launch video filmed between a rental location in Brooklyn and on campus at NYU with a small crew in a single day.
